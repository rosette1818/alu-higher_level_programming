The ``5-text_indentation`` module
===================================

Import the function:

    >>> text_indentation = __import__('5-text_indentation').text_indentation

Regular case, no special characters:

    >>> text_indentation("Holberton School")
    Holberton School
    <BLANKLINE>

Case with a period:

    >>> text_indentation("Holberton. School")
    Holberton.
    <BLANKLINE>
    School
    <BLANKLINE>

Case with a period, a question mark, and a colon:

    >>> text_indentation("Holberton. School? How are you: John")
    Holberton.
    <BLANKLINE>
    School?
    <BLANKLINE>
    How are you:
    <BLANKLINE>
    John
    <BLANKLINE>

Empty string:

    >>> text_indentation("")
    <BLANKLINE>
